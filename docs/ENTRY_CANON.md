# ENTRY_CANON

## Purpose

This file explains the ten-document entry canon for the repository.

The goal is not to claim that these ten documents contain the whole repository.
The goal is to identify the smallest set that reveals its center of gravity.

## The Ten-Document Entry Canon

### 1. `protocol_core/canons/CANON_INDEX.md`

This is the cleanest doorway into the canonical Spektre layer.
It shows the canons as a system rather than as isolated files.

### 2. `protocol_core/core/1=1.md`

This is the invariant seed.
It names the minimal consistency condition that the rest of the repository repeatedly builds on.

### 3. `protocol_core/core/PROTOCOL.md`

This is the shortest direct statement of what Spektre v1.1 is as a protocol.
It anchors the repository in a state-first and responsibility-bound frame.

### 4. `protocol_core/core/UNITY.md`

This clarifies that unity here means coherence, not uniformity.
It is one of the best bridge-documents between formal invariant and lived/system meaning.

### 5. `protocol_core/core/SOVEREIGN_AGENCY.md`

This is one of the clearest documents on state ownership, decision ownership, and non-delegable responsibility.
It makes the human side of the protocol explicit.

### 6. `protocol_core/core/STATE_BEFORE_INTERPRETATION.md`

This states one of the repository’s most important ordering rules.
It explains why explicit state must come before narrative, meaning, or explanation.

### 7. `protocol_core/core/STATE_COMMIT.md`

This is the strongest single document on commit, irreversibility, and consequence-bearing action.
It turns the protocol from abstract principle into execution logic.

### 8. `formal_structure/system_models/SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md`

This places the protocol core into a wider structural and systems frame.
It is one of the best orientation documents for understanding how Spektre relates to state spaces, expressivity, and execution layers.

### 9. `execution_system/core/SUPERKERNEL.md`

This exposes the execution layer in a compact and readable way.
It shows what the repository is trying to protect when it talks about action, ownership, and non-delegable decision power.

### 10. `human_layer/relationships/HUMAN_NETWORK.md`

This prevents the repository from being misread as a purely abstract or technical artifact.
It shows how the protocol ultimately points back to human relations, meaning, and real-world consequences.

## Why These Ten Together

Taken together, these ten documents expose five things:

- invariant
- protocol
- agency
- execution
- human meaning-preservation

That combination is why they form the strongest entry canon.

## What They Do Not Replace

These ten documents do not replace:

- the formal mathematical layer in `formal_structure/formalism/`
- the broader execution layer in `execution_system/`
- the recovery and integration layer in `human_layer/`
- the AI/substrate boundary layer in `ai_interface/`
- the applied and exploratory shelves

They are only the best first compression.

## Suggested Use

If someone asks, “Where should I start?”
this file and the ten documents above are the shortest serious answer.
