# REPO_MAP

## Repository Identity

This repository is a layered conceptual archive.

Its center is `Spektre v1.1`, but the repository as a whole is larger than the protocol itself. It contains:

- canonical protocol documents
- formal structure and system models
- execution and control material
- human-layer material
- AI/substrate-boundary texts
- applied protocols
- essays
- exploratory conceptual documents
- archive-preserved historical and parallel material

The repository is therefore best understood as a knowledge graph with a canonical center, not as a flat document dump and not as a conventional codebase.

## Final Structural Takeaway

The repository now has a visible center of gravity.

- `protocol_core/` and `formal_structure/` hold the strongest canonical and near-canonical core
- `execution_system/`, `human_layer/`, and `ai_interface/` make the major operational layers legible
- `applied_protocols/` separates practical modules from theory
- `essays/` now exposes its own thematic clusters instead of remaining one undifferentiated applied shelf
- `explorations/` and `archive/` preserve breadth without hiding the nucleus

The point of the structure is not to shrink the repository.
It is to reveal its layers.
